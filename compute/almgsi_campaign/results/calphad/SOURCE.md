Generated by `calphad/calphad_receiver.py` (run 2 Oct 2026 on the DGX Spark,
aarch64; total wall time about 3 min for three temperatures).

## Settings

- Temperatures 300, 453, 803 K; P = 101325 Pa; N = 1 mol of atoms.
- Receiver value: dG_f(x, T) = G_eq(x, T) - [x_Al G(fcc Al) + x_Mg G(hcp Mg) +
  x_Si G(diamond Si)] at the same T and P, in eV/atom (1 eV/atom = 96485.33 J/mol).
  G_eq is the pycalphad equilibrium (global minimum) Gibbs energy at the overall
  candidate composition read from `candidates/<cid>.xyz`. Components are the
  elements present plus VA, so the pure elements are evaluated as unaries.
- Phase set: all 37 phases buildable from {AL, MG, SI, VA} (pycalphad
  `filter_phases`), including LIQUID, minus GAS and minus AL12MG17 (the Al-Li-Mg
  sublattice model of gamma, which has no Al-Mg end-member parameters; gamma
  Al12Mg17 is ALMG_GAMMA). Liquid is retained everywhere and appears only where it
  is stable (12 of 31 compositions at 803 K, none at 300 or 453 K).
- Cross-check with only the phases assessed for Al-Mg-Si and its binaries
  (LIQUID, FCC_A1, HCP_A3, DIAMOND_A4, MG2SI, ALMG_BETA, ALMG_EPS, ALMG_GAMMA):
  identical stable assemblages at every composition and temperature, maximum
  value difference 1.4e-10 eV/atom. The extra phases never become stable.
- `wall_s` is the time of one pycalphad equilibrium call for that composition
  (0.4 to 3.8 s); candidates sharing a composition carry the wall time of the
  single shared evaluation.

## Coverage

- Campaign pool (`calphad_T{T}.json`): all 180 formation_energy candidates,
  status ok. They span **27 distinct compositions**: CALPHAD describes phases,
  not atomic orderings, so all orderings of one composition (for example the 29
  candidates at Al2MgSi) receive the same value.
- Observed phases outside the pool (`calphad_observed_T{T}.json`): 4 candidates
  (beta'' Mg5Si6, beta' Mg9Si5, gamma Mg17Al12, epsilon Mg23Al30), evaluated at
  their overall compositions (31 distinct compositions in total).
- 10 cluster_binding candidates are not covered (not a formation energy).

## Sanity check against QE PBE (`results/live_values.json`, qe_pbe)

Kendall tau-b of the CALPHAD value against the PBE formation energy on the 179
shared candidates (no PBE value for mp_1079297_Si), and against the lowest PBE value per
composition (27 compositions):

| T (K) | tau vs PBE, per candidate (p) | tau vs HSE06, per candidate | tau vs lowest PBE per composition (p) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 0.065 (0.21) | 0.084 | 0.172 (0.21) |
| 453 | 0.065 (0.21) | 0.084 | 0.172 (0.21) |
| 803 | 0.175 (0.0008) | 0.190 | 0.247 (0.073) |

The 300 K and 453 K orderings are identical because the stable assemblages do
not change between these temperatures. Low tau is expected: PBE scores each
ordering (most suggested fcc decorations lie well above the PBE hull), while
CALPHAD scores the equilibrium assemblage at the composition, which is always at
or below zero. For example, the 19 Al4Mg3Si candidates have PBE values from
+0.021 eV/atom upward, while CALPHAD gives -0.088 eV/atom (fcc + beta + Mg2Si).
Al-Si compositions are 0 in CALPHAD (fcc + diamond, negligible solubility) but
+0.05 to +0.21 eV/atom for the ordered PBE structures.

```bash
VIRTUAL_ENV=<venv> uv pip install pycalphad==0.11.2 ase
<venv>/bin/python calphad/calphad_receiver.py
```
